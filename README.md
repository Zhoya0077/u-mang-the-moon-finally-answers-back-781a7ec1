The Moon finally answers back: one line, 111 characters, in the voice of a wry, four-billion-year-old Moon tired of being wished upon — and pointing out that nobody ever wished it anything back.

> Four billion years of wishes, howls, and 2 a.m. promises — and not one of you has ever wished ME anything back.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

The program writes the entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`) and prints it to stdout, after a self-checking gate (1–240 characters, exactly one line, no control characters).
